4. YOUR FILES

The Software copies files from a folder on your PC into a folder on your Android
phone and can overwrite files in the phone folder you choose. If you turn on the
optional two-way sync (on both the PC and the phone), it also copies changes from the
phone into the shared PC folder, can overwrite files there, and - if you also turn on
"Sync deletions too" - can delete files on either side (files deleted on the PC go to
the Recycle Bin; files deleted on the phone are permanently deleted). When both sides
change a file, both versions are kept. You are responsible for backing up your data.
The author is not responsible for lost, overwritten or deleted files.

5. NETWORK AND INTERNET USE

Folder Sync PC runs a small server on your PC that serves one folder to phones that
scanned its QR code (a secret pairing code and the PC's certificate fingerprint are
part of the code). It can be reached on your local network or, if you choose
"Internet", from anywhere through your PC's public IP address. Please note:
  - Over the internet or on public Wi-Fi, transfers can be slow, and they may use
    mobile data for which you are charged by your provider. That cost is yours.
  - "Internet" mode opens a port on your router (UPnP) or needs you to forward one,
    and exposes the shared folder to anyone who has the pairing code while the app
    is running (and, with two-way sync on, lets them add, change and delete files). Keep the code private, use "New code" to revoke old pairings, and
    share only folders you are happy to expose. It may not work on connections that
    share one public address.
  - Traffic is encrypted with TLS using a self-signed certificate that the phone
    app pins; this reduces, but does not remove, the risks of exposing a server.
Windows may ask you to allow the app through the firewall.
